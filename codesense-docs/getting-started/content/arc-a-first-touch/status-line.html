Status line

The status line is one line on the right of the title bar where CodeSense and your agents say what they just did: an agent opened a file, a layout change you can undo, a workspace that is still loading. You read it there, act on it there, and it goes away on its own.

What shows there

  • Undo lines: something just changed and you can take it back, with the key that undoes it shown after the button.
  • Offers: an agent did something you may want to look at, with a button such as Show.
  • Progress: a line that updates in place while something loads, then ends with its final line.
  • Announcements and notes: a short message such as Nothing to undo. These show one after another, in the order they came.

How long it shows

A line shows for 6 seconds; an Undo line shows for 10. Point at the line or move focus into it and it waits; leave it and it carries on with the time it had left.

Undo comes first

An Undo line, from you or from an agent, shows at once, ahead of any offer. A newer Undo line replaces an older one. The offer it covered comes back afterwards with the time it had left.

+N waiting

When more offers arrive than fit, they wait behind +N waiting ▾. Open it to see each waiting offer with its action and a ✕ to dismiss it. Up to three offers wait; a fourth drops the oldest, and an offer that has waited a minute goes away. Each waiting offer gets its full time when it shows.

Acting on it

  • Press Tab to reach the line's button; it runs once and the line closes.
  • Press Esc inside the line to close it. Focus goes back to where it was.
  • In +N waiting ▾, the arrow keys move between offers and Esc closes the menu.
  • Screen readers hear each line once, when it shows, from one polite live region named "status line".
