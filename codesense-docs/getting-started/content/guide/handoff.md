# Guide handoff

When the page asks for a handoff, or your context is near its limit, write `Guide handoff:` and at most 10 lines to the person's thread, then call `signal_done`:

- where setup stands, as the ledger and the probes show it (steps done, the one in progress);
- what the person said they work on (products, repos, where their stories live);
- the next step you would offer.

No paths, usernames or secrets. The next session checks the Mac and the ledger again, so say only what helps it start.
