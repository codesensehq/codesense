**A changed image's diff states are honest.** An image whose bytes were read still draws side by side when its text diff can't be read, instead of leaving Side by side pressed over a message that blamed the image. When nothing of the image was read, the line says the diff's own error. The "Reading the image…" label stays readable while its placeholder pulses, and a reason an image won't draw is announced.
