**Shell keys reach the terminal off macOS.** With a terminal focused, Ctrl+letter, Ctrl+Space and Ctrl+\ go to the shell and app shortcuts on them use Ctrl+Shift instead; copy and paste are Ctrl+Shift+C and V, and AltGr characters are no longer shortcuts. When a Ctrl+Shift chord is taken, the moved shortcut wins and the other one works once focus leaves the terminal; Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] stay with the shell.
