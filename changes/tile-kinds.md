**Any tile holds any content as tabs.** With the tiles preview on, a tile can hold dashboards, files, story pages, diffs, Notes, the Inbox, agents and shells, each as a tab in one tab bar with its own controls. A dashboard in a tile reads as its own team, reveals and focuses in its tile, and has the same access as in the right pane. A terminal stays in one place at a time.
