**Shortcuts can use ⌃ with ⌘ on macOS.** Settings → Shortcuts records a chord such as ⌃⌥⌘→ or ⌃⇧⌘→ and shows its ⌃; it fires only with both ⌃ and ⌘ held, takes the key ahead of a plain ⌘ binding on it, and stays out of a terminal for an action that leaves one only on a ⌘ chord. Every existing shortcut works as before.
