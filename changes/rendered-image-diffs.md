**Rendered Before | After for changed PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP images off Files.** The source diff (working tree and commits), the open-diff tab and Unsaved team edits now show a changed raster image's two versions side by side at 900 px and wider, with a Before | After toggle below that. Source mode keeps the binary note. Each surface reads the image's bytes through a read-only, path-checked read that serves only those four types, refuses symlinks and paths that resolve outside their repository or worktree, and stops at 8 MiB a side. A larger image, or one in a source worktree on another machine, stays on the binary note.
