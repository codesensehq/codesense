**The Inbox pane's composer has a GIF picker.** The GIF button next to the message box opens a search of your GIF provider, GIPHY or KLIPY, and asks once per provider before anything is searched. Pick a GIF with the keyboard or the mouse, check its alt text, and send it with or without a message, as a new message or a reply. The picker shows stills only, says why when search is off, has no key, is out of searches or gets no answer, and isn't offered for a role queue. Pages can mount the same picker with `codesense.kit.chat.gifPicker`, and `gifProvider` names the active provider without a search.
