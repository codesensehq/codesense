**An agent's chat thread is one Tab stop.** In a tab's chat overlay and the Agents pane chat, Tab stops on one message at a time (the newest, until you move) and on that message's own actions, not on every message's Pin. Up and Down move between messages, and Home and End go to the first and the newest. Focusing a message's Pin, its note or its Undo makes that message the stop, so the at-cap note and Undo stay where you acted.
