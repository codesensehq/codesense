**Turning GIF search off or changing a key stops pings sooner, and one provider's key no longer resets the other.** A GIPHY view ping already queued no longer goes out after you turn GIF search off or replace or remove the GIPHY key, and a failed id request from before the change no longer delays the next send. Saving or removing one provider's key now leaves the other provider's held searches and sends alone.
