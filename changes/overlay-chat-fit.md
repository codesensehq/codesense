**An agent's chat keeps its composer and its room on small panes.** Opening a pin on a short pane no longer removes the composer, and Esc in an empty composer closes the pin. Small and short panes get a one-row composer, so the thread keeps more lines. The composer's hint, placeholder and key words now meet AA contrast, the thread's focus ring is the 2 px accent outline, and key hints are 11 px. An empty chat says "No messages yet" once, and a failed refresh names the chat. In `kit.chat`, a page's empty copy waits for the thread's first read: a held read says "Loading the conversation…", and a failed one keeps its alert, now with Retry.
