**The live hub publishes one needs-you set on a new `needsYou` topic, and every count of what needs you reads it.** The set holds every question to you, permission ask, merge card, and agent that is capped at its spend cap or has failed; FYI messages never count, and an agent that ended failed or crashed stays until its mark is dismissed. Each row names its kind, session, org, team and work item, a team's view holds only that team's items, and subscribing takes the inbox read grant. It is recomputed when sessions, the Inbox or spend change, with no polling. `codesense_overview` gains a `needsYou` section counted by kind and narrowed to a team agent's own team; its `sessions.needsYou` lists the sessions those items are about, or says `error` when the set could not be read; its routines `needsYou` count is renamed `runsAwaiting`, since runs waiting on you are not items of the set. The Inbox counts row's `needsYouBySender` counts each sender's cards in the set, and a sender with nothing waiting drops out of the map.
