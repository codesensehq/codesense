**The agent chat composer is one two-row box.** On agent pages the message box holds the text on top and its hint and Send under it. Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line; ⌘↵ (Ctrl↵) sends too, with Shift held as well. On a short or narrow pane it falls back to one line beside Send, and the Enter that confirms an input-method candidate no longer sends.
